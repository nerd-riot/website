# Nerd Riot — Visual Identity & Brand System

> **A riot of tools for developers who give a damn.**  
> Small tools. Sharp edges. No unnecessary bullshit.

---

## 1. Brand Personality & Tone

Nerd Riot is a collective workshop for engineers building opinionated, lightning-fast developer tools. We are:
- **Playful, nerdy, slightly rebellious** — but never sloppy or unprofessional.
- **Engineering-first** — we care about latency, memory footprints, keyboard shortcuts, and clean architecture.
- **High-contrast & minimal** — zero corporate fluff, zero meaningless enterprise buzzwords.
- **Welcoming to open-source contributors** — clear docs, pragmatic standards, mutual respect.

## 3. Typography Direction

- **Headings & Display:** Clean, modern geometric sans-serif (`-apple-system`, `SF Pro Display`, `Inter`, `Geist Sans`) set with tight letter-spacing (`-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` to `900`).
- **Code & Brand Accents:** Monospace with character (`JetBrains Mono`, `SF Mono`, `Fira Code`) for prompts, benchmarks, and badges.
- **Body Text:** Neutral, highly readable typography with relaxed line height (`1.6`).

## 5. Usage Rules

- **Do:** Use solid dark backgrounds (`#0A0D14` or `#0D1117`) with crisp geometric contrast.
- **Do:** Maintain clear whitespace around the mark (at least 20% margin).
- **Don't:** Apply fuzzy generic AI gradients, photorealistic 3D textures, or cartoon mascots.
- **Don't:** Distort aspect ratios or rotate the glyphs off-axis.
